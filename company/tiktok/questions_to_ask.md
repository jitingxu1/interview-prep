# Questions to Ask — TikTok

Organized by round. Pick 3–4 per conversation; ask the shaping ones early so you can aim your answers.

---

## HM intro call (and HM deep dive)

**Ask early — you need these to aim everything else:**

1. **"Where is the agentic platform today — zero to one, or consolidating tools teams have already built? And who are its main customers: internal engineering teams, ops, or creators directly?"**
2. **"What does the 30-person team look like right now — how many ICs vs. managers, and how much is still to be hired?"**
   Tells you whether you'd manage managers and how much of the job is hiring.
3. **"Is this a new seat or a backfill? Why now?"**

**Middle:**

4. **"How is work split between the San Jose team and the teams in China and Singapore? Where do roadmap decisions get made?"**
   The single most important question for this role. Listen for whether US owns direction or executes it.
5. **"What does success look like at the end of year one?"**
6. **"Of framework, harness infrastructure, and tool ecosystem — which is the most urgent problem right now?"**
   Then engage with the answer technically.
7. **"How technical do you want this manager to be — in design reviews, or fully on strategy and people?"**

**Close:**

8. **"What do managers who do well in PGC have in common?"**

---

## Head of PGC

- "How does the agentic platform fit into PGC's priorities for next year? What would make it a top priority vs. a supporting one?"
- "Where do you see the biggest leverage for agents in professional content creation?"
- "How do you think about build vs. adopt for agent infrastructure — internal framework vs. open-source or vendor?"

## Head of short video

- "If the agent platform worked exactly as you hoped, what would your teams be able to do that they can't today?"
- "What's frustrated your teams most about working with platform teams in the past?"
  Gives you the exact failure mode to promise you won't repeat.

## HRBP

- "What's the actual working rhythm for this team — how often are there late-evening syncs with China or Singapore?"
- "How does performance review work at the manager level here? How often, and what's weighted most?"
- "What do people who leave this team in the first year usually say?"

## For Helen (recruiter), not interviewers

- Level and band for the role, in writing, before the loop.
- Vesting schedule, refreshers, sign-on terms.
- Whether the loop can be compressed if you get another offer.

## Don't ask interviewers

- Comp, level, or title — that's Helen.
- Anything about TikTok's US regulatory situation, unless they bring it up.
- Generic "room for growth?" — question 8 covers it better.
