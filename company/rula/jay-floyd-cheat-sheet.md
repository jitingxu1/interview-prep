## Step 1 — Leadership design

- [x] ★ **Q1. Your leadership style?**
  - Hint: situational → four modes → match the skill, not the title → tell them where they are → incident (directive) + pod leads (delegate) → over-coaching trap
  - Key: "A senior engineer can *need* directing on something brand new."
- [x] ★ **Q2. What changed from tech lead to manager?**
  - Hint: value by doing → by building the system → design, staff, watch, fix → meeting load 50% → ~20% → two-week test
  - Key: "If I'm out for two weeks, does the team slow down? If it does, the system isn't done yet."
- [x] **Q3. Team and culture you want to build?**
  - Hint: context + safety → clarity → transparency by default → safety to disagree and fail → healthy way = easy way
  - Key: "Culture is what you protect when it costs you something."

## Step 2 — Leadership drag

- [x] ★ **Q4. A time you were the bottleneck?** ⚠️ numbers, team shape
  - Hint: "I was the bottleneck" → four areas, all syncs → the design, not my time → owners + written decision rights + co-run → sit in → step out → still watch it
  - Key: "The problem wasn't my time, it was the design."
- [x] ★ **Q5. Biggest weakness?** ⚠️ one real result
  - Hint: the answer man → telling and doing (1:1s, the POC) → cost: they don't grow, I miss their ideas → "what do you think?" + "is this only I can do?"
  - Key: "The instinct hasn't gone away. But now I notice it before I act on it."
- [ ] **Q6. A belief you changed your mind on?**
  - Hint: having the answer → building the system; feedback early, not softened
  - Key: "The discomfort of feedback early is much cheaper than the damage of giving it late."

## Step 3 — Delegation and ownership

- [x] **Q7. Delegate without lowering the bar?**
  - Hint: define good → bar in the system (design doc, tests, eval gate) → oversight matches risk → eval pipeline 30 → 5 min
  - Key: "The standard is enforced by the system, not by my calendar."
- [ ] **Q8. When to step in on a technical decision?**
  - Hint: inside one area and reversible → stay out; crosses teams, hard to reverse, another org's metrics → step in
  - Key: "Ownership matters more than the extra five percent."

## Step 4 — Performance and hard conversations

- [x] ★ **Q9. Underperforming engineer?**
  - Hint: never first heard at review → early patterns → clarity / fit / support / personal → written plan → backend engineer → infra → fit talk + HR
  - Key: "Whatever happens, nobody is surprised and it's fair."
- [x] ★ **Q10. A difficult conversation?** ⚠️ cause, result
  - Hint: tech lead's manager path cancelled → prepared (facts, what to offer, his feelings, fast) → news in the first minute → acknowledge → sub-team, no date → readiness and scope
  - Key: "Don't tie someone's growth plan to a title or a date you don't control."
- [x] **Q11. Managed someone out?**
  - Hint: honest no → backend engineer (one line) → clarity/fit/support → written plan + HR → dignity → won't avoid it
  - Key: "Keeping someone in a role they can't succeed in isn't kind to them."
- [ ] ★ **Q12. Leading former peers?** ⚠️ needs a real story, or "haven't, here's how I would"
  - Hint: name the shift → don't pull rank → let work convince → no surprises
  - Key: "Pretending the relationship hasn't changed is what breaks trust."
- [x] **Q13. Two people in conflict?** ⚠️ hybrid, result
  - Hint: diagnose the type → architecture deadlock → separately first → one-page docs, criteria first → hybrid → propose, debate, commit
  - Key: "A conflict often looks technical but is really about who gets heard or who decides."

## Step 5 — Calm authority

- [x] **Q14. Trust with a team that didn't choose you?** ⚠️ result
  - Hint: inherited 10 → listen first → one visible win → never miss a promise → no surprises → absorb the noise
  - Key: "People judge a new manager by whether the first promise survives pressure."
- [ ] **Q15. Leading under pressure?**
  - Hint: support automation: 67% escalation, 30-min reviews, 500 tickets / 10K → name it honestly → one target, not ten → protect focus → 67% → 50%+, 30 → 5 min, 500 → 260
  - Key: "Under pressure, the manager's job is to narrow, not widen."
- [ ] **Q16. Communicating bad news?**
  - Hint: facts early → the plan → what I need from you → steady, not anxious → refer to Q10
  - Key: "Transparency is the facts and the plan. Dumping is passing my stress down."
- [ ] **Q17. Your energy and rhythm?**
  - Hint: weekly owners' sync, growth-focused 1:1s, a focus day → track decisions coming to me → when it climbs, fix delegation
  - Key: "Pressure doesn't go away; the system is what absorbs it."

## Coaching and leveling

- [x] ★ **Q18. How you coach; how you know it works?**
  - Hint: where they want to go + what energizes them + peer input → one or two areas → stretch work → growth 1:1s → three signals
  - Key: "They bring me decisions they've already made, not questions."
- [x] ★ **Q19. Leveling and promotions, fairly?**
  - Hint: transparent → plan (strengths, readiness and scope) → evidence → calibrate (peer input, notes all cycle, rotate stretch work)
  - Key: "A promotion should recognise work that's already happening."
- [x] ★ **Q20. Coach vs. give the answer?** ⚠️ one real example
  - Hint: urgent → answer · they can find it → coach · in between → bounded room · asked twice → help · watch for dependence
  - Key: "They walk out with their own reasons to move, not mine."
- [x] ★ **Q21. Who coaches you?** ⚠️ confirm mentor
  - Hint: mentor, HR partner, my own team → specific asks + reflection loop → feedback story → change
  - Key: "If I'm asking them to be coachable, I have to model it."
- [ ] **Q22. Calibrating how much direction?**
  - Hint: follows the skill, not the title → confidence collapses = over-coaching; stuck and silent = under-directing (see Q1)
  - Key: "Calibration is a weekly judgment, not a fixed style."
- [ ] **Q23. Helping a struggling peer manager?** ⚠️ needs story
  - Hint: exchange notes, not a lecture → 1:1s with everyone in your perimeter → coaching is only part of the job
  - Key: "Systems: delegation, expectations in writing, hard conversations on time."

## Recruiting and growing teams

- [ ] **Q24. A team you built from scratch?**
  - Hint: 12 engineers, two pods → first hires are the culture → standards on day one → give decisions away early → would write decision rights sooner
  - Key: "Bottleneck at 12, liability at 20."
- [x] **Q25. How do you hire?** ⚠️ best-hire story
  - Hint: scorecard → split areas → written feedback first → scope, not years → culture add → raise the average
  - Key: "If I'm not sure, it's a no."
- [ ] **Q26. Someone you promoted?** ⚠️ needs story
  - Hint: who, the gap, the stretch, what changed in them (see Q19; or the Q10 tech lead, one line)
  - Key: "The difference between hoping someone grows and actually growing them."

## Cross-team coordination and roadmaps

- [ ] **Q27. Roadmap with unclear goals?** ⚠️ confirm 40% → 60%
  - Hint: work backward from the business problem → current vs. goal → what's blocking → roadmap in business terms (fraud chart: one line, home is Q33)
  - Key: "We're solving real problems, not building cool things."
- [ ] ★ **Q28. A cross-org initiative?** ⚠️ teams, timeline, one moment of resistance
  - Hint: 60+ fraud models → 1:1s with everyone first → one eval, one serving path, one set of ownership rules → underwriting $1M → $25M
  - Key: "People don't hand over their system to someone who hasn't understood it."
- [ ] **Q29. Saying no to a stakeholder?** ⚠️ confirm the chargeback story
  - Hint: an alternative, not a wall → say what the boundary protects → hold it when it costs you
  - Key: "Saying no while helping them solve it."
- [ ] **Q30. Vague goals from above?**
  - Hint: one measurable target → name the decision, the assumption, the revisit date → own the ambiguity upward
  - Key: "Clarity isn't handed to you; a manager creates it."
- [ ] ★ **Q31. AI adoption and guardrails?** ⚠️ needs one real example
  - Hint: paved path with evals + shadow runs → safe path = easy path → humans on high-stakes calls → measure real use
  - Key: "Adoption isn't a training problem, it's a design problem."
- [ ] **Q32. Leading through a big change?**
  - Hint: the why before the how → earn trust before you ask → one system to rally around
  - Key: "Change lands when people understand the why before they give up the how."

## Alignment and positioning

- [x] ★ **Q33. Making work visible to leadership?**
  - Hint: two things: how you say it (business first; fraud chart) → who says it (engineers) → where → does it land
  - Key: "If the work is strong but the message isn't landing, that's my problem to fix."
- [ ] **Q34. Burnout vs. misalignment?**
  - Hint: same symptoms, opposite fixes → load: subtract (protect focus) → fit: move the seat (backend engineer, one line)
  - Key: "Burnout needs subtraction; misalignment needs a different seat."
- [ ] **Q35. Competing values (action vs. empathy)?** ⚠️ needs story
  - Hint: name the tension → agree when each wins → both are strengths
  - Key: "The action person is why things ship; the empathy person is why people stay."
- [ ] ★ **Q36. Priorities: long-term vs. short-term?**
  - Hint: two principles → reserve long-term capacity every week → weekly by impact → ~70/30 agreed together → short-term built from long-term milestones
  - Key: "This week's priorities are steps toward the long-term outcome, not a different list."
