14. **[Underperformance](./underperformance.md)** — the "how do you handle an underperforming engineer?" answer

    - Home of the backend-engineer story; ~90-second answer, checklist, six follow-ups with quick hints, practice log

15. **[Difficult Conversation](./difficult_conversation.md)** — the "tell me about a difficult conversation" answer
    - Telling a tech lead his manager path was cancelled by an org change: preparation, the first minute, a careful promise
    - Lesson: build growth plans around readiness and scope, not a title or date; four follow-ups with hints

16. **[Coach vs. Answer](./coach_vs_answer.md)** — the "when do you coach, and when do you just give the answer?" answer
    - Three questions: urgency/stakes, do they have what they need, bounded room; the "asked twice" rule; dependence as the warning sign
    - Follow-ups with hints, including a framework and template for "a time coaching didn't work"

17. **[Coaching and Mentoring](./coaching_and_mentoring.md)** — the "how do you coach your engineers, and how do you know it's working?" answer
    - Understand (goals, energy, peer input) → plan (one or two areas, stretch work) → rhythm; three signals it's working
    - Frameworks and hints for: someone who doesn't know what they want, a goal that doesn't fit the team, mentoring seniors who know more

## How to Use

- Review these before interviews to internalize your philosophy
- Reference when answering "what's your management style?" or "how do you think about X?"
- Use Q&A as a foundation and personalize answers for each company
