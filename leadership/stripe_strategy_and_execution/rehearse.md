# Rehearse these out loud

[← Round 2 index](README.md)

- [ ] The foundation story, told in two minutes, with real numbers in the blanks
- [ ] The same story retold as a leverage story for Q15, not a pushback story
- [ ] One project walkthrough end to end: goal → plan → risks → tracking → what slipped → outcome
- [ ] A metric you got wrong, and what you changed
- [ ] A goal you missed, and how you handled the review
- [ ] A replan mid-quarter: what the early signal was and how fast you acted
- [ ] A resource you asked for and didn't get — what you did instead
- [ ] Q10a: a senior request you pushed back on — the first question you asked, the option you offered, and what happened (best if you were overruled and committed)
- [ ] Your capacity split (committed / investment / reserve) with the actual percentages you used
- [ ] Which cross-functional teams you worked with, one example each

**Two answers to have ready regardless of the question asked:**

1. **When you go deep versus delegate.** One example of each, and the rule you use to decide. This round is partly titled "details execution" for a reason, and the distinction is what separates depth from micromanagement.
2. **The thing that is currently broken in how you run your team.** Interviewers at this level read a candidate with no self-criticism as either inexperienced or guarded. One honest, specific limitation — and what you are doing about it — is worth more than another success.

**Last pass before the round:** for every story in this doc, say the rejected alternative out loud in one sentence. If you cannot, the story is not ready.
