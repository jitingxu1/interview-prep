# Manager Interview Question Bank

A practice list. Not company-specific — use it for any engineering manager loop.

**How to practice**: pick a question, start a 2-minute timer, answer out loud without notes. If you run past 2 minutes, you're rambling. If you finish under 45 seconds, you're too thin. The follow-ups under each question are what a good interviewer asks next — practice those too, because that's where most candidates fall apart.

Story references (`→ S1`, `→ S4`) point to files in [`../star_stories/`](../star_stories/).

---

## 1. Building, Supporting & Scaling Teams

1. Tell me about a team you built from scratch. What did you get right, what would you do differently? → S1, S4
   - *How did you decide the first three roles to hire?*
   - *What did the team look like at month 3 vs. month 18?*
2. Walk me through how you scaled a team. What broke as it grew? → S4
   - *At what headcount did your management approach have to change?*
3. How do you define a "high-performing team"? How do you know when you have one?
   - *What's your leading indicator that a team is about to underperform?*
4. Tell me about the best engineer you ever hired. How did you find and assess them?
5. Tell me about a hire that didn't work out. What did you miss?
   - *Did you change your interview process afterward?*
6. How do you run 1:1s? Tell me about a 1:1 that changed something.
7. Tell me about someone you promoted. What did you do that made the difference? → S7
   - *Would they have gotten there without you?*
8. Tell me about someone on your team who was struggling. → S8, S9
   - *How long did you give it before you acted?*
   - *What would you have done if the repositioning hadn't worked?*
9. Have you ever had to manage someone out? Walk me through it.
10. How do you handle a strong performer who is a bad teammate?
11. Tell me about a time you lost someone you didn't want to lose.
    - *What did the exit conversation tell you?*
12. How do you build a team where people feel safe disagreeing? → S6
13. What's your approach to leveling and calibration? How do you keep it fair?
14. How do you develop engineers who don't want to be managers?
15. How do you retain people when you can't promote them or pay them more?
16. How do you onboard a new engineer so they're productive fast?
17. Describe your team today. Who are the strongest people and why?
18. How do you handle a team that's demoralized after a project gets cancelled?

## 2. Execution, Tradeoffs & Ambiguity

19. Tell me about the hardest tradeoff you've made. → S2
    - *Who disagreed with you? What did they say?*
    - *Knowing what you know now, was it the right call?*
20. Tell me about a time you had to make a decision with incomplete information. → S2
    - *What would have changed your mind?*
    - *How did you decide it was time to stop gathering data?*
21. Describe a project that slipped. What happened and what did you do? **(prepare this — commonly asked)**
    - *When did you first know it would slip? What did you do that day?*
    - *How did you tell your stakeholders?*
22. Tell me about a project you killed or de-scoped.
23. How do you set a roadmap when the goals are still being defined?
24. How do you plan when you don't control your dependencies?
25. Tell me about a time you had to reprioritize mid-quarter. How did you communicate it?
26. How do you decide what your team says no to? → S3
27. Tell me about a production incident you led through.
    - *What changed structurally afterward?*
28. How do you balance shipping fast against building the right foundation?
29. Tell me about technical debt you chose to take on deliberately. How did you pay it back?
30. How do you measure whether your team is executing well?
    - *What metrics do you actually look at weekly?*
31. Tell me about a time you were wrong about a technical direction. → S5
32. Your team is committed to more than it can deliver. What do you do?
33. How do you operate when the strategy above you keeps changing?
34. Tell me about something you shipped that didn't have the impact you expected.
    - *How did you find out? What did you do with that?*
35. How do you protect focus time while staying responsive to the business? → S3

## 3. Cross-Functional Partnership & Influence

36. Tell me about your relationship with your Product partner. How do you divide ownership?
    - *Where do you disagree most often?*
37. Tell me about a time you disagreed with Product and won. And a time you lost.
    - *How did you commit to a decision you disagreed with?*
38. Tell me about influencing a team you had no authority over. → S10
    - *What was your first move? What did you give up to get the yes?*
39. Describe a cross-org initiative you drove. → S10
    - *How many teams? How long did alignment take?*
40. How do you get buy-in from a partner team that has no incentive to help you?
41. Tell me about a conflict with a peer manager. How did it resolve?
42. How do you push back on an executive request without damaging the relationship?
43. Tell me about a time you had to say no to a senior stakeholder. → S3
44. How do you translate technical tradeoffs for a non-technical audience?
45. Tell me about a time cross-functional misalignment cost you real time. What did you change?
46. How do you keep stakeholders informed without drowning your team in meetings? → S3
47. Tell me about how you've influenced beyond your own team — a practice, standard, or process that spread.
48. How do you handle a partner team that consistently misses commitments to you?
49. How do you work with your own manager? What do you escalate and what do you absorb?

## 4. Technical Leadership as a Manager

50. How technical are you day to day? How do you stay credible without being a bottleneck?
51. Tell me about a technical decision you personally made or overruled.
    - *How did the team react?*
52. When do you get involved in a design, and when do you stay out?
53. How do you evaluate the technical quality of work you didn't do yourself?
54. Tell me about the most complex system your team owns. Walk me through the architecture.
    - *What's the part you'd redesign?*
55. How do you keep learning technically while managing?
56. What's your view on managers writing production code?
57. How do you handle a senior engineer whose technical judgment you disagree with? → S6
58. What ML/AI problems is your team solving, and why are they hard?
59. How do you think about build vs. buy, especially for AI infrastructure?
60. How do you evaluate and adopt new AI tooling on your team without chasing hype?

## 5. Growth, Self-Awareness & Motivation

61. Walk me through your career. Why each move? **(the opener — nail this)**
62. Why are you leaving your current role? **(highest-risk answer — see `../self-introduction/`)**
63. Why this company? Why this team?
64. What's the biggest mistake you've made as a manager? → S5
    - *What specifically do you do differently now?*
65. What feedback have you received that was hard to hear?
    - *Who gave it to you? Did you agree?*
66. What are you actively working on improving right now?
    - *What's your evidence it's getting better?*
67. Where do you want to be in three years? Manager of managers, or something else?
68. What kind of manager do you want to become that you aren't yet?
69. What would your direct reports say is your biggest weakness?
70. What would your last manager say about you?
71. What's the hardest part of management for you personally?
72. What energizes you at work? What drains you?
73. What's a belief about management you've changed your mind on?
74. What do you need from a manager to do your best work?
75. What would make you say no to this job?

## 6. Values, Culture & Judgment

76. Tell me about a difficult conversation you had to have. How did you prepare?
77. Tell me about feedback you gave that didn't land. → S5
78. How do you build trust with a team that didn't choose you as their manager? → S1
79. Tell me about a time you had to deliver bad news to your team.
80. How do you handle it when your team disagrees with a decision from above?
81. Tell me about a time you advocated for someone.
82. How do you think about diversity when building a team?
83. Tell me about an ethical or risk call you had to make.
84. What's something you believe about engineering leadership that most people disagree with?

## 7. The Curveballs

85. What questions do you have for me? **(never say "none" — have 4 ready)**
86. What haven't I asked that I should have?
87. Sell me on yourself in 60 seconds.
88. What's your biggest professional regret?
89. If I called your last three reports, what's the one thing they'd all say?
90. What are you better at than 90% of managers?

## Practice Protocol

**Round 1 — coverage.** Go through each section, answer every question once at low effort. Mark the ones where you had nothing. Those are your gaps.

**Round 2 — depth.** Take the marked questions. Find a real story for each. Write four bullets: situation, what you did, result, what you learned. Don't write a script.

**Round 3 — pressure.** Answer with the follow-ups. Have someone ask "why?" three times in a row. That's what a senior interviewer actually does.

**Round 4 — timing.** Record yourself. 2 minutes for a story, 60–90 seconds for a philosophy question. Listen back once.

### What gets people rejected at this level
- Saying "we" when asked what *you* did
- No numbers — scope, headcount, dollars, latency, timeline
- Stories with no failure, no tension, and no cost
- Not knowing what you'd do differently
- Being unable to name a real weakness
- Describing the last company as if the interviewer knows the internal context
