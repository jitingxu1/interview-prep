# Q4. Tell me about a strategic hire you made and why

[← Round 1 index](README.md)

The word doing the work here is **strategic**. They are not asking about a good hire; they are asking whether you hire against a plan. A strategic hire is one where the *role* was your idea — you saw a gap in the team's capability and created the headcount to close it, rather than backfilling a departure.

**The frame to use:**

1. **The gap I saw.** What the team could not do, and what that was costing. ("We could build models but nobody owned the serving path, so every launch bottlenecked on one person.")
2. **Why a hire and not something else.** The alternatives you rejected: train someone internally, borrow from a platform team, contract it out. Say why each was worse.
3. **What I was willing to trade.** Strategic hires usually mean giving something up — a different open role, a slower quarter, a higher level than planned. Name it.
4. **How I found and evaluated them.** Especially if the profile was unconventional — a career switcher, a weaker resume with strong loop signal, someone from an adjacent domain.
5. **What changed afterward.** The capability the team has now that it did not have before, with a number if you have one.

**Fill in:** which hire this was, what the gap was, and what you gave up to make the role exist.

**How they grade it:**

| Signal | Strong | Red flag |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Origin | Created the role from a gap they spotted | A backfill relabeled as strategic |
| Alternatives | Rejected train / borrow / contract, with reasons | Hiring was the only option considered |
| Cost | Names what was traded to get the headcount | Headcount appeared for free |
| Outcome | A capability the team has now, with a number | "They're great" with nothing that changed |

**Follow-ups to prepare:**

- "Why that level? Could a mid-level engineer have done it?"
- "Who else was in the final round, and why did you pick this person over them?"
- "Did the hire work out the way you expected? What surprised you?"
- "How did you convince leadership to approve the headcount?" (This one overlaps with Q16 on advocating for resources — reuse the material.)
