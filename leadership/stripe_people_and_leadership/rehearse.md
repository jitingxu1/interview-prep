# Rehearse these out loud

[← Round 1 index](README.md)

Read silently, these all feel answered. Said out loud, half of them stall. Time yourself: two minutes per story, not five.

- [ ] Q1: judgment first, then emotional stability — plus one judgment call and one composure story, each with the specific moment it mattered
- [ ] Q2: the hire where team composition, not raw strength, decided it
- [ ] Q3: the role you left open rather than lowering the bar — and what it cost
- [ ] Q4: the strategic hire, including what you traded to create the role
- [ ] Q5: the first sentence of a performance conversation, word for word
- [ ] Q6: your first ninety days, framed as what you would do at Stripe
- [ ] Q7: influence without authority — and why you chose not to escalate
- [ ] Q8: both conflicts, plus the line about when not to intervene
- [ ] A hire you got wrong, and the process change that followed
- [ ] For every story above: the rejected alternative, in one sentence

**Questions to ask them.** Prepare three, and make at least one of them about how the team actually runs:

- How does performance management work here in practice, and how often do teams get reshaped? (The direct version of the attrition question — asked neutrally, it reads as diligence.)
- What does the strongest manager on this team do that the others don't?
- What would you want me to have changed six months in?
