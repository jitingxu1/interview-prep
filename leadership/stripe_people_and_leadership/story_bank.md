**The gaps only you can fill — do these before anything else:**

- Numbers. For each story: how long, how many people, how much it moved. If you cannot measure it, say that explicitly in the answer rather than reaching for a vague claim.
- The rejected alternative. One sentence per story on what you did not do and why.
- The exact words. For the people stories — poor performer, conflict, influence — one real sentence you said out loud. This follow-up comes almost every time.
- "What I'd do differently." One honest line per story. Not a humblebrag; a real limitation you saw afterward.

Two stories worth having even though no question names them directly: a decision you got **wrong** and what you changed afterward, and a time you **recognized invisible glue work** in a review or promotion case. Senior-manager signal shows up in both.
