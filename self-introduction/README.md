# Self-Introduction & Background

Your personal pitch, background arc, and key narrative threads.

## Purpose

This folder contains materials for answering:
- "Tell me about yourself"
- "Walk me through your background"
- "Why did you get into tech/management?"
- "What excites you about this role?"

## Versions

1. **[Short Version](./short_version.md)** — 30-60 seconds
   - Quick elevator pitch
   - Use when: Quick intro, time is limited

2. **[Long Version](./long_version.md)** — 2-3 minutes
   - Full story with depth
   - Use when: You have time to tell the complete arc

3. **[Manager Version](./manager_version.md)** — Emphasize leadership & people
   - Focus on team building, development, organizational impact
   - Use when: Interviewing for manager/leadership roles

4. **[Tech Lead Version](./tech_lead_version.md)** — Emphasize technical depth
   - Focus on systems thinking, architecture, technical decisions
   - Use when: Interviewing for tech lead or architect roles
