# Why Leave DoorDash: Short Version (30-45 seconds)

**For quick answers to "why are you looking?" or "why leave?"**

---

Honestly, I've learned a lot at DoorDash, but I'm not super passionate about the delivery space. The margins are thin, and there's a ceiling on how much the business can grow. I'm more drawn to startup environments where you can build a product with less legacy code, less organizational baggage, and more room for innovation. I want to work somewhere that can move faster and has bigger growth potential ahead.
