# Why Leave DoorDash: Long Version (1-2 minutes)

**For deeper conversations when interviewers ask about motivation**

---

I've had a great experience at DoorDash. I've built teams, shipped real impact, worked on hard problems. But honestly, I'm not that passionate about the delivery space itself. 

The reality is, the margins in delivery are really thin. You're competing on speed and cost, and there's a natural ceiling to how much the business can grow and how much value you can create. After a few years, you start to see that ceiling, and it's not super motivating.

What I'm really drawn to is working in a startup environment again. I want to build products where there's less legacy code, less organizational burden, and more room to actually innovate. At DoorDash, we're managing a massive platform—which is valuable, but it also means you're constrained by a lot of existing decisions, existing infrastructure, existing culture.

In a startup, you get to make different choices. You can design systems from scratch, you can move faster, you can experiment. And there's more upside—the growth potential feels real and exciting, not just incremental.

That's what I'm looking for in my next role. A place where I can build something that feels new, where the growth trajectory is steep, and where my technical decisions aren't constrained by years of legacy. I want to be part of something that feels like it's just getting started.
