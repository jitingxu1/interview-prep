**For quick intros, elevator pitches, or when asked "tell me about yourself"**

Hi, I'm Jiting. I'm currently a tech lead and manager at DoorDash in our opex org, and I'm leading the ML and AI team—that's about 12 engineers split across a couple of pods: predictive ML, agentic AI, and our risk data platform. We're focused on three main areas: customer support automation, fraud detection, and abuse prevention.

What's been really exciting is building and leading this team from scratch. My role covers a lot—I'm thinking about the long-term technical vision, the planning and roadmaps, hitting milestones, training and growing the junior engineers, handling cross-functional alignment with product and business, and then also the hiring and coaching. So it's really about making sure we have the right people, they're growing, we're hitting our milestones, and we're executing on a solid technical vision.

Before DoorDash, I worked at VoltronData and claypot as an engineering (lead) manager, where I led development of some open source projects—Ibis, which is a portable Python dataframe library that lets you write code once and run it across 20+ different data engines, and Ibis-ML for building scalable machine learning preprcossing pipelines. Ibis is something like pandas and ibis-ml is something like scikitlearn - preprocessing, the only differenece is they are more scalable, could run it on cluster for big dataset. 

And before that, I spent time at Airbnb as a tech lead, built the location graph—everything from data collection to ranking and understanding. I also worked on user engagement, figuring out how to send the right content to the right users at the right time.

So yeah—I've done the full spectrum. I'm an engineer who became a tech lead, and now I'm managing teams while staying hands-on with the technical work.
